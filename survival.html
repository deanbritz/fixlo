Your three fixes

How this is worked out
  • We run your cash forward week by week for a year, once for each shock. Normal trading resumes after a short shock ends.
  • When sales fall, your stock and materials spend falls with them. Everything else (wages, rent, loans, drawings) keeps going.
  • Losing your biggest customer: their sales stop for the year and their last month of invoices goes unpaid.
  • Sales drop: 30% lower for six months.
  • Key person away: sales fall by your answer for four weeks.
  • Cyber lockout: sales halve while you're locked out. Two days if you have tested backups and two-step sign-in, a week if backups are untested, three weeks otherwise. IT recovery costs aren't included, so the real hit is likely larger.
  • Supplier failure: sales halve until you've replaced them.
  • Each fix is tested by rerunning all five shocks with that one change.

This is a quick stress test built on your own estimates. It isn't financial advice.

Everything is worked out on your device. Your figures are never sent or saved. Only the feedback you choose to send reaches FIXLO.
